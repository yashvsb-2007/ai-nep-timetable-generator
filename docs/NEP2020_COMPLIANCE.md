# NEP 2020 Multidisciplinary Compliance Framework

This document outlines how the AI Timetable Generation System satisfies the mandates of the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 for Higher Education Institutions.

## Core Features Supported

1. **Multidisciplinary Course Baskets**:
   - **Major Courses**: Core disciplinary specialization.
   - **Minor Courses**: Secondary cross-department stream (e.g. B.Tech CSE with Minor in Data Science or Humanities).
   - **Multidisciplinary Electives**: Courses open to all students across faculties (e.g. Ethics in AI, Climate Science).
   - **Skill Enhancement Courses (SEC)**: Hands-on vocational skills.
   - **Ability Enhancement Courses (AEC)**: Language & communication skills.
   - **Value Added Courses (VAC)**: Wellness, Indian Knowledge Systems, Environmental studies.

2. **Academic Bank of Credits (ABC)**:
   - National ABC ID verification & credit transfer tracking.
   - Semester credit caps: 16 minimum to 24 maximum credits per semester.

3. **Constraint Solver Rules for NEP**:
   - Synchronized timetable slots for cross-department elective baskets so students from any major can attend open electives without schedule clashes.
